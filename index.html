Not Another Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Not Another Sudoku is developed by Konstantinos Koronellos ("I", "me", or "the developer"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and this privacy-policy page. For privacy questions or requests, contact notanothersudoku@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores settings, saved puzzles, game progress, statistics, achievements, cosmetic unlocks, favorites, and game history on your device. Custom background images and cached Game Center player information may also be stored. These records support gameplay, personalization, and restoring your progress.

You do not need to create an account with the developer to play solo puzzles. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites or sell your personal information.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable iCloud sync, supported app records are stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit service and synchronized between your devices. These records can include progress, settings, game history, favorites, and custom background images. Apple handles your Apple Account and the operation of iCloud; the developer does not receive your Apple Account password.

You can turn iCloud sync off in the app's Settings. Turning it off stops synchronization on that device and keeps local data available; it does not itself delete existing iCloud records. You can manage the app's iCloud storage through Apple's account and device settings.

Game Center and multiplayer

If you use Game Center features, the app uses Apple-provided player identifiers, display names, profile images, achievements, and gameplay information to provide those features. During Race a Friend and co-op matches, player identity and relevant match information, such as puzzle details, moves, progress, timing, and results, are exchanged through Apple's Game Center services with the other player. Match history may retain player identifiers, names, images, and results locally and, when enabled, in your private iCloud database.

Other players can see the Game Center identity and match information needed for the multiplayer feature. You can manage your Game Center profile and privacy preferences in Apple's settings.

Photos, camera, and notifications

If you choose a photo background, the app receives the image you select through Apple's photo picker and stores a processed copy for personalization. That copy may be included in private iCloud sync and exported backups. The developer does not receive it automatically.

Camera access is used to scan puzzle and invitation codes. Scanning is processed on your device; the app does not send camera footage to the developer. You can revoke camera permission in your device settings.

With your permission, the app schedules local Daily Challenge and streak reminders. You can disable these in the app or your device's notification settings.

Backups and sharing

You can export app data to a backup file and choose where to save or share it. Backups can contain settings, progress, saved games, history, Game Center player information, and custom background images. Treat backup files as private. Files and puzzle or invitation links you choose to share are handled by the destination service and any recipients you select.

Diagnostics and support

The app keeps rotating diagnostic logs on your device to help investigate errors. Logs are not automatically uploaded to the developer. You can manage detailed logging, delete logs, or export diagnostics through the app's support and diagnostics controls.

If you send a support email, I receive your email address, the message and any attachments you choose to send. The app's support composer can attach a diagnostics archive containing technical events and error information. Support correspondence is processed through email providers, including Gmail, to respond to your request and investigate problems.

Apple may separately process usage or crash diagnostics under your device's analytics-sharing choices and Apple's policies.

Retention and your choices

App records remain on your device or in your private iCloud storage until removed or replaced. Diagnostic files rotate as their storage limit is reached, and you can delete them in the app. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Removing the app does not necessarily remove iCloud records, device backups, exported files, or information already shared with another player.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle requests, investigate related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact me to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held in support correspondence. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object to processing, restrict processing, request portability, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority.

The purposes of processing are to provide the features you request, honor permission choices for optional features, and address support and reliability issues. Where data protection law requires a legal basis, these activities rely, as applicable, on providing the requested service, your consent, or legitimate interests in responding to support requests and maintaining the app. Apple, GitHub, and email providers may process information in countries outside your own under their respective policies and safeguards.

Children

The app does not ask children to submit personal information to the developer. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact me so I can address it.

This website and service providers

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no developer-added analytics, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the hosting service.

For information about these providers' practices, see Apple's Privacy Policy, GitHub's Privacy Statement, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be available on this page.
