Need help with Not Another Sudoku for iPhone or iPad? Contact the developer, Konstantinos Koronellos, for help, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests.

Contact support

Questions, feedback, or something not working as expected?

Email Support notanothersudoku@gmail.com

You can also open Settings → Support & Diagnostics → Email Support in the app to prepare an email with diagnostic logs attached. Review the message and attachment before sending.

Report a problem

To help investigate your report, please include:

  • The app version and build number, shown in the app's Settings.
  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The steps to reproduce the issue, if you know them.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if helpful, with personal information removed.

Never send passwords, verification codes, or Apple Account credentials. A full game backup is not needed for an initial support request.

Common questions

Where can I find the game instructions?

Open Settings → How to Play in the app for the Sudoku rules and solving guidance.

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is optional and uses your private iCloud storage. To sync between devices, use the same Apple Account and enable iCloud sync in the app on each device. The app's Settings includes iCloud sync status and diagnostics. Turning sync off keeps local data and stops synchronization on that device; it does not delete existing iCloud records.

How can I keep a backup of my progress?

Use the backup export and import options in the app's Settings. Keep exported backup files somewhere private: they can contain progress, settings, history, and custom background images. Before troubleshooting a progress issue, export a backup if possible.

What do Race a Friend and co-op require?

Multiplayer uses Apple's Game Center and requires an internet connection. Check that both players are signed in to Game Center and are using the latest available version of the app. If a connection fails, include any displayed message in your support request.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes. Send your idea to the support email above and explain how it would improve your experience.
